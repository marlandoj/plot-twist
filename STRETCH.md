# STRETCH (not in the build)

Scope was frozen on day one: 6 x 4 grid, 5 buildings, 12 wish rule types, weekday ramp
(Monday easiest, Sunday hardest). Everything below is deliberately out.

- Image comments: render the 300px SVG snapshot to PNG and attach it to the comment via media
  upload. The text-and-emoji map is the shipped (guaranteed) format.
- Drag-to-place (tap-to-place only today).
- "Ghosts" of other players' towns on your board after you lock in (heat map of popular lots).
- Friends or subreddit-vs-subreddit leaderboards; all-time leaderboard view.
- Sound and haptics.
- Automatic suggestion approval (vote threshold plus a profanity and name filter). The MVP uses
  manual operator approval from the mod menu.
- A second puzzle size or a "hard mode" with 6 buildings.
- Localised wish phrasings.
- Archive mode for replaying past days (Reddit posts already act as the archive).
- Per-user flair colour picker.
